Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 (analytics and crash reports)

The short version: Pebblesip has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your water logs, goal and settings stay on your phone. Free users see ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID, subject to the consent choices described below.

This policy explains how Pebblesip: Water Reminder ("Pebblesip", "the app") handles information. The app is published by SALMAN S SHAIKH, an individual developer based in India ("I", "me").

1. Information stored only on your device

The following is stored locally on your phone and is never sent to me:

  • Drinks you log (amount, drink type, time) and your daily totals and streaks.
  • Your profile for the goal calculation: weight, activity level and climate.
  • Your settings: daily goal, units, wake-up and bedtime, reminder preferences, cup sizes, fasting mode settings, app and widget theme.

Home-screen widgets and reminder notifications read this local data on your phone. If you export your data as a CSV file (Premium), the file is created on your phone and shared only where you choose to send it.

You can erase all of it at any time in Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Location (optional, Ramadan mode only)

If you use Ramadan mode and choose "Use my approximate location", the app asks for Android's approximate (coarse) location permission. It is used only on your phone to calculate prayer times (Maghrib and Fajr), rounded to about 1 km, and stored locally. It is never sent to me or anyone else. You can instead pick a city from a list, and the app works fully with location denied. Pebblesip never requests precise or background location.

3. Notifications

Water reminders are scheduled on your phone. Notification permission is optional.

4. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows a small banner on the Stats screen and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch to try a widget theme. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interactions, to show, measure and (where permitted) personalise ads and to prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before ads load. You can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Premium users see no ads.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases (Premium)

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. I never see your payment details. Purchase status is managed by RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts and basic device information so that Premium can be unlocked and restored. See the RevenueCat privacy policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Usage analytics and crash reports

To understand which features are used and improve the app, Pebblesip uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record a small set of usage events, such as "a drink was logged" (with whether it came from the app, a widget or a notification), "the goal was met", "a widget was added", "fasting mode was turned on", "the paywall was shown" and "a purchase was completed". Firebase also records basic device and app information (device model, Android version, app version, country derived from IP) and an app-instance ID.

  • Events never include your drink amounts, goal, weight, location or other logged data.
  • Firebase Analytics is set up without the advertising ID and without ad personalisation.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics only runs if you allow it in the consent form; you can change this in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

See the Firebase privacy information.

If the app crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent to Sentry (stored in the EU) so I can fix it. It contains the error details, the app and Android version, device model and recent app events (such as screens opened), but no drink logs, goal, weight, location or other personal data. See the Sentry privacy policy.

7. What I don't do

  • No sign-up, no account, no cloud sync.
  • I don't sell your personal information.
  • I don't collect your health data. Pebblesip does not connect to Health Connect or Google Fit.

8. Health disclaimer

Pebblesip is a habit tracker for staying hydrated. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Talk to a doctor about your own fluid needs, especially if you are pregnant, an athlete, or have kidney, heart or other medical conditions.

9. Children

Pebblesip is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly: view it in the app, export it (Premium), or delete it with Settings → Delete all data. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, use their tools or contact them. Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the GDPR), you may have further rights; contact me and I will help.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
